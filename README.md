## My Favorite Series: Tokyo Ghoul

**Genre:** Dark fantasy, Horror, Action

**Studio:** Pierrot

> A college student becomes half-ghoul after a surgery and has to live between two worlds.

### Why I Like It
- The story is emotional and keeps you thinking
- The main character changes a lot over time
- The soundtrack and opening song are memorable
- It shows that people can be both good and bad

### Main Characters
1. Ken Kaneki
2. Touka Kirishima
3. Hide Nagachika
4. Yoshimura
5. Kishou Arima
